# Direction research

Mechanism: a crowd changes a composite mascot while a caller guides an operator through three discrete controls.
Scene: lively college workshop; first delivery is solo laptop rehearsal.
Avoid both the generic quiz dashboard and its predictable neon hacker-console opposite.

Grounded candidates in resonance order:
1. Character press: screen-print workshop and interchangeable toy moulds; three ingredients visibly produce one figure.
2. Television cue board: shared programme timing and large character reveal.
3. College festival print wall: bold local event graphics and collective participation without borrowed religious motifs.
4. Science-exhibit bench: directly manipulable cause and effect.
5. Flipbook animation desk: frames expose changing character construction.
6. Arcade control deck: discrete tactile input and immediate feedback.
7. Pocket theatre: caller as director, operator as puppeteer, audience as casting crew; cue strips and character staging.

Seed ee0edab4 assigns candidate 7. The user chooses among it, candidate 1, a competitive creator-hardware direction, or an explicitly subordinate conventional option. Challenger verdicts and transferable disciplines are recorded in direction-options.json.
